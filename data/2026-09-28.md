### [1] [Counterdiabatic quantum optimization for efficient state preparation in the Schwinger model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31400)
*Ethan Laval,Bipasha Chakraborty,Stefano Cipolla*

Main category: hep-lat

TL;DR: This paper systematically analyzes the DC-QAOA, a variant of the Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm that uses counterdiabatic driving to improve ground-state preparation for the Schwinger model, showing it reduces circuit depth and improves efficiency over standard QAOA.

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: The motivation is to enhance the efficiency and effectiveness of quantum algorithms, specifically for preparing ground states of gauge theories, by reducing the circuit depth and runtime through the use of counterdiabatic driving in the QAOA framework.

Method: The method involves a theoretical and computational analysis of DC-QAOA and other counterdiabatic variants, benchmarking their performance against the standard QAOA when applied to the ground state preparation of the (1+1)-dimensional Schwinger model, a key lattice gauge theory.

Result: The results demonstrate that incorporating counterdiabatic driving in QAOA leads to improved ground-state preparation and lower circuit depth requirements compared to the standard QAOA, indicating a more efficient approach to quantum simulation of gauge theories.

Conclusion: The conclusion is that counterdiabatic protocols, such as DC-QAOA, offer a practical and efficient means for the quantum simulation of gauge theories, suggesting potential advancements in the field of quantum computing and its applications.

Abstract: The Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm (QAOA), whose design is underpinned by the adiabatic theorem, is one of the leading variational quantum algorithms for preparing the ground states of gauge theories. A recently proposed variant, DC-QAOA, incorporates counterdiabatic driving to accelerate the adiabatic process, reducing both circuit depth and runtime. In this work, we present a systematic analysis, from both theoretical and computational perspectives, of DC-QAOA and related counterdiabatic variants for preparing the ground state of the Schwinger model, the paradigmatic (1+1)-dimensional lattice gauge theory. Benchmarking these methods against standard QAOA, we show that counterdiabatic driving improves ground-state preparation while lowering circuit depth requirements. Our results establish counterdiabatic protocols as a practical route towards efficient quantum simulation of gauge theories.
