### [1] [A Vector-Vector-Axial Anomaly in 4D](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17369)
*Evan Berkowitz,Shi Chen,Aleksey Cherman*

Main category: hep-lat

TL;DR: The paper discusses a 4D purely-bosonic modified Villain model that could have a direct continuous phase transition, potentially leading to a 4D conformal field theory (CFT) with a vector-vector-axial (VVA) anomaly.


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: The motivation is to explore the use of the modified Villain discretization, which provides exactly-integer topological quantities, as a means to achieve correct anomaly structure at finite lattice spacing, specifically for an ABJ-type vector-vector-axial anomaly in a 4-dimensional model.

Method: The author presents a 4-dimensional purely-bosonic modified Villain model. The approach involves the application of the modified Villain discretization to ensure the preservation of topological properties and to facilitate the study of anomalies within the context of lattice field theories.

Result: The result is the proposal of a 4D bosonic model that may exhibit a direct continuous phase transition between two spontaneously broken symmetry (SSB) phases, suggesting the potential for this model to give rise to a 4D CFT with a VVA anomaly.

Conclusion: The conclusion is that the modified Villain model presented offers hope for the existence of a 4D CFT with a VVA anomaly, due to the possibility of a direct continuous phase transition from one SSB phase to another.

Abstract: Because it offers exactly-integer topological quantities, the modified Villain discretization offers a route to correct anomaly structure at finite lattice spacing. I will present a 4-dimensional purely-bosonic modified Villain model with an ABJ-type vector-vector-axial anomaly.
  The model may have a direct continuous phase transition from one SSB phase to another, giving hope of yielding 4D CFT with a VVA anomaly.
