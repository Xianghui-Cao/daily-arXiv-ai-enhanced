### [1] [Entanglement spectrum ordering and flavor polarization in the two-flavor Schwinger model at vacuum angle $θ= π$](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14296)
*Boliang Yu,Ruixin Zhou,Meisen Gao*

Main category: hep-lat

TL;DR: The paper examines the entanglement spectra in the two-flavor Schwinger model at a vacuum angle θ=π, revealing how the joint Schmidt distribution reflects gauge-sector hierarchy and flavor asymmetry, which are influenced by the cut position and mass imbalance. The analysis separates gauge-sector ordering, sector weight, and flavor polarization, which are otherwise mixed in the globally ordered entanglement spectrum.


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: The motivation is to understand how the entanglement spectra can provide information about symmetry breaking and the organization of gauge sectors within the context of the two-flavor Schwinger model, particularly focusing on the role of the vacuum angle, subsystem gauge charge, and flavor imbalance.

Method: The method involves analyzing the joint Schmidt distribution p_QA,FA, which is characterized by the subsystem gauge charge QA and flavor imbalance FA. By defining combined weights and conditional polarizations, the researchers investigate the effects of different cuts and mass imbalances on the gauge-charge and flavor distributions.

Result: The results show that the gauge-charge distribution changes with the staggered cut, and mass imbalance breaks the FA↔-FA symmetry of the conditional flavor distribution. Moreover, the weight hierarchy and conditional polarization respond differently to the cut position and mass imbalance, allowing for the separation of various physical aspects within the entanglement spectrum.

Conclusion: The study concludes that symmetry resolution through the analysis of the entanglement spectra can disentangle the gauge-sector ordering, sector weight, and flavor polarization, providing a more detailed understanding of the underlying physics in the two-flavor Schwinger model.

Abstract: Entanglement spectra in gauge theories can encode both symmetry breaking and the organization of gauge sectors. In the two-flavor Schwinger model at vacuum angle $θ=π$, we find that the joint Schmidt distribution $p_{Q_A,F_A}$, labeled by the subsystem gauge charge $Q_A$ and flavor imbalance $F_A$, reveals a cut-dependent gauge-sector hierarchy and a mass-induced flavor asymmetry: changing the staggered cut reorganizes the gauge-charge distribution $p_{Q_A}$, while mass imbalance breaks the $F_A\leftrightarrow-F_A$ symmetry of the conditional flavor distribution $p_{F_A|Q_A}$. Defining the combined weight $W_F^{(q)}=p_{q,+1}+p_{q,-1}$ and conditional polarization $\mathcal P_F^{(q)}=(p_{q,+1}-p_{q,-1})/W_F^{(q)}$, we find that changing the cut reverses the weight hierarchy, $W_F^{(-1)}>W_F^{(+1)}$ at unit-cell boundaries but $W_F^{(+1)}>W_F^{(-1)}$ at intra-cell cuts, while mass imbalance drives $\mathcal P_F^{(q)}$ away from zero with a $q$-dependent cut response. Symmetry resolution therefore separates gauge-sector ordering, sector weight, and flavor polarization that are mixed in the globally ordered entanglement spectrum.
