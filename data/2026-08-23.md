### [1] [The QCD crossover temperature and curvature coefficient from unbiased exponential resummation at physical quark masses in (2+1)-flavor lattice QCD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19330)
*Sabarnya Mitra*

Main category: hep-lat

TL;DR: The paper applies an unbiased exponential resummation method to determine the QCD pseudocritical temperature, $T_{\rm pc}$, and shows consistency with previous lattice-QCD results. It also provides a new approach for probing the QCD crossover at small finite baryon density.

Motivation: The motivation is to apply the unbiased exponential resummation method for the first time to determine the QCD pseudocritical temperature at zero baryon chemical potential, and to explore its consistency with expected scaling behavior and previous results from lattice QCD.

Method: The authors use the unbiased exponential resummation method to reconstruct the finite-density baryon number susceptibility, $\chi_2^B$, and define two thermal-derivative observables. They then analyze the peak positions of these observables and the fourth-order baryon number susceptibility, $\chi_4^B$, to determine the pseudocritical temperatures and the curvature coefficient, $\kappa_2^B$.

Result: The study yields pseudocritical temperatures $T_{\rm pc}^T=160.2(4)(3)$ MeV and $T_{\rm pc}^C=158.1(5)(3)$ MeV, which are consistent with previous lattice-QCD results. The leading second order curvature coefficient, $\kappa_2^B$, is estimated using scaling relations between $\chi_4^B$ and the thermal derivatives of $\chi_2^B$. An independent determination of the curvature is also provided through the analysis of the $\mu_B$ dependence of the pseudocritical temperatures, which remains statistically consistent with the scaling-based estimates.

Conclusion: The unbiased exponential resummation method is shown to be consistent with the expected scaling behavior and it provides a complementary approach to investigate the QCD crossover at small finite baryon density. The results are in agreement with previous lattice-QCD findings, reinforcing the reliability of the new method.

Abstract: We present the first application of the unbiased exponential resummation method to the determination of the QCD pseudocritical temperature $T_{\rm pc}$ at vanishing baryon chemical potential $μ_B$. By reconstructing the finite-density baryon number susceptibility $χ_2^B$, we define two thermal-derivative observables whose peak positions yield $T_{\rm pc}^T=160.2(4)(3)$ MeV and $T_{\rm pc}^C=158.1(5)(3)$ MeV. We also show that the pseudocritical temperature, $T_{\rm pc}^χ$ obtained from the peak of the fourth-order baryon number susceptibility $χ_4^B$ is consistent with these determinations and with previous lattice-QCD results based on Taylor expansions \cite{Bollweg:2022Pade}. Further, we demonstrate that scaling relations \cite{Bollweg:2022fqq} between $χ_4^B$ and the thermal derivatives of $χ_2^B$ yield mutually consistent estimates of the leading second order curvature coefficient $κ_2^B$ at the corresponding pseudocritical temperatures. A direct analysis of the $μ_B$ dependence of the pseudocritical temperatures provides an independent but substantially less constrained determination of the curvature, which remains statistically consistent with the scaling-based estimates. These results demonstrate the consistency of unbiased exponential resummation with the expected scaling behavior and establish it as a complementary approach for probing the QCD crossover at small finite baryon density.
