### [1] [Equation of state of hot and dense QCD using the complex Langevin equation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32385)
*Michael Mandl,Dénes Sexty,Daniel Unterhuber*

Main category: hep-lat

TL;DR: The paper presents QCD equation of state results at high temperatures and large baryon chemical potentials, achieved through complex Langevin simulations with physical pion mass. It includes findings on baryon density, pressure, and trace anomaly under specified conditions.


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: To provide a more accurate understanding of the QCD equation of state at extreme conditions, which is essential for astrophysics and heavy-ion collision experiments, by performing simulations with physical parameters that have not been previously achieved.

Method: The authors used complex Langevin simulations, a stochastic method, to compute the QCD equation of state at the physical pion mass. The simulations were conducted in a strangeness-neutral, charm-neutral, and isospin-symmetric environment.

Result: Results for the baryon density, pressure, and trace anomaly as functions of baryon chemical potential and temperature are presented, providing new insights into the behavior of QCD matter under these conditions.

Conclusion: The study provides continuum-extrapolated lattice data for the QCD equation of state, extending our knowledge to higher baryon chemical potentials than previously accessible, and sets a foundation for further theoretical and experimental studies in this field.

Abstract: We present continuum-extrapolated lattice results for the QCD equation of state at high temperatures and unprecedentedly large baryon chemical potentials. These results were obtained by performing the first complex Langevin simulations at the physical value of the pion mass. We work in a strangeness-neutral, charm-neutral, and isospin-symmetric setup and show results for the baryon density, pressure, and trace anomaly as a function of baryon chemical potential and temperature, respectively.
