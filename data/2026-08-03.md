### [1] [Generalized parton distributions from lattice QCD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29574)
*Krzysztof Cichy,Martha Constantinou*

Main category: hep-lat

TL;DR: The chapter reviews recent progress in extracting generalized parton distributions (GPDs) from lattice QCD, covering theoretical principles, two main lattice approaches, and their results, and discusses future prospects.


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: To provide an overview of the recent advancements in the extraction of GPDs from lattice QCD, highlighting the theoretical background and the current computational methods used to achieve this.

Method: The chapter explains two primary lattice QCD approaches for GPD extraction: Ji's quasi-distributions and Radyushkin's pseudo-distributions. It then reviews the results obtained from these methods.

Result: A comprehensive review of the current state of GPD extraction using lattice QCD, including the outcomes from both Ji's and Radyushkin's methodologies, is presented.

Conclusion: The chapter concludes with a discussion on the future directions and potential developments in the field of GPD extraction from lattice QCD.

Abstract: This chapter gives an overview of the recent progress in extracting generalized parton distributions from lattice QCD. We briefly recall the theoretical principles of GPDs and explain the two most common lattice approaches, Ji's quasi-distributions and Radyushkin's pseudo-distributions. In the second part of the chapter, we review the lattice results obtained from these two frameworks. Finally, we offer a discussion of future prospects of lattice extractions of GPDs.
