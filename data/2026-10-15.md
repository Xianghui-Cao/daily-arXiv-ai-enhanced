### [1] [Excited-State Effects in Nucleon Sigma Terms from a Variational Analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20362)
*Constantia Alexandrou,Giannis Koutsou,Yan Li,Srijit Paul,Ferenc Pittler*

Main category: hep-lat

TL;DR: The paper examines the excited-state effects in nucleon sigma-terms within lattice QCD, using twisted-mass clover-improved fermion ensembles. It uses gauge ensembles with quark masses close to their physical values and shows that by using an optimal combination of interpolating operators, the time dependence of the light sigma-term can be reduced. The study also applies Laplace filtering and observes minimal excited state contributions for both strange and charm sigma-terms.

Motivation: The motivation is to better understand and quantify the excited-state effects on the nucleon sigma-terms in lattice QCD simulations, which are important for precise calculations of nucleon properties and interactions with mesons.

Method: The method involves the use of twisted-mass clover-improved fermions, multiple gauge ensembles with near-physical quark masses, and the solution of a generalized eigenvalue problem derived from correlator matrices. Additionally, Laplace filtering is applied to assess residual excited-state contributions.

Result: Results indicate that using the optimal linear combination of interpolating operators significantly reduces the time dependence of the light sigma-term without sacrificing statistical precision. For the strange and charm sigma-terms, the excited-state contributions are observed to be small over a range of source-sink separations, with the charm term showing no significant time dependence due to large statistical uncertainties.

Conclusion: The study concludes that employing an optimal combination of interpolating operators and Laplace filtering helps to minimize the excited-state contamination in the calculation of nucleon sigma-terms, leading to more reliable results.

Abstract: We investigate excited-state effects in nucleon sigma-terms in lattice QCD using twisted-mass clover-improved fermion ensembles. We study correlation functions with nucleon and multihadron nucleon-$σ$-meson interpolating operators. The analysis is performed using three gauge ensembles, including one ensemble simulated with two degenerate light, a strange, and a charm quark with quark masses tuned to approximately their physical value. We solve the generalized eigenvalue problem that arises from the matrix of correlators and show that the three-point function for the light sigma-term obtained using the optimal linear combination of interpolating operators has substantially reduced time dependence without loss of statistical precision. We apply Laplace filtering and investigate residual excited-state contributions in the extraction of $σ_{πN}$. Using the same optimal linear combination of correlators as for $σ_{πN}$ for the strange sigma-term we also observe suppressed excited state contributions over an extended range of source--sink time separations. For the charm sigma-term, we observe negligible time dependence within the large statistical uncertainties, and these improvements show no significant effect.
