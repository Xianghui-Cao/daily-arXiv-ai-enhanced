### [1] [QCD Chiral Crossover Line from Lee-Yang Edge Singularities](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05752)
*Heng-Tong Ding,Swagato Mukherjee,Peter Petreczky,Kai-Fan Ye*

Main category: hep-lat

TL;DR: A new method for reconstructing the QCD chiral crossover line is proposed, utilizing Lee-Yang edge singularities and lattice QCD data. The method shows consistency with existing results and provides a framework for future improvements.

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: The motivation is to develop a novel approach that uses universality and complex baryon chemical potential plane to map out the QCD chiral crossover line, which can be applied to high-precision lattice QCD data and provide a consistent constraint on the crossover line.

Method: The method involves using lattice-extracted Lee-Yang zero estimates as proxies for edge singularities and mapping them to the universal chiral Lee-Yang edge. This allows for the determination of the μ_B dependence of the chiral critical line and the pseudo-critical crossover line at physical quark masses.

Result: The reconstructed curvature of the crossover line is found to be consistent with existing continuum lattice-QCD results at small μ_B, and the transition temperature in the chiral limit aligns with previous chiral-scaling analyses. The method provides a quantitatively consistent constraint on the QCD crossover line within the current temperature range.

Conclusion: The study demonstrates that combining lattice information on Lee-Yang singularities with universal chiral scaling can effectively constrain the QCD crossover line, offering a promising framework for further refinements with additional data.

Abstract: We propose a universality-based reconstruction of the QCD chiral crossover line from Lee-Yang edge singularities in the complex baryon chemical potential plane. The framework maps lattice-extracted complex Lee-Yang-zero estimates, treated as proxies for edge singularities, to the universal chiral Lee-Yang edge and thereby determines the $μ_B$ dependence of both the chiral critical line in the light-quark chiral limit and the pseudo-critical crossover line at physical quark masses. As an illustration, we apply the framework to Lee-Yang-zero estimates recently obtained by the Wuppertal-Budapest collaboration from high-statistics lattice QCD simulations. Without imposing the previously determined small-$μ_B$ expansion of the crossover line as input, the reconstructed curvature is consistent with existing continuum lattice-QCD results at small $μ_B$. The fitted chiral-limit transition temperature is also compatible with existing chiral-scaling analyses. These results demonstrate that lattice information on Lee-Yang singularities, combined with universal chiral scaling, provides a quantitatively consistent constraint on the QCD crossover line within the present temperature window and establishes a framework that can be systematically improved with future Lee-Yang-zero determinations.
