### [1] [Searching for symmetric mass generation with staggered fermions in four dimensions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18239)
*Nouman Butt,Simon Catterall,Gwen Hartshaw,Anna Hasenfratz*

Main category: hep-lat

TL;DR: The paper explores the phase diagram and critical behavior of a lattice Higgs model with two massless staggered fermion fields and a scalar field, finding a massless symmetric phase at weak coupling, a massive symmetric phase (SMG) at strong coupling, and an anti-ferromagnetic phase in between. A unique multicritical point is identified where fermion bilinear condensates vanish but susceptibilities diverge.

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: The motivation is to understand the phase structure and critical phenomena of a lattice Higgs model that includes fermions and a scalar field, which could provide insights into the nature of symmetry breaking and topological defects in quantum field theories.

Method: Numerical simulations are used to map out the phase diagram by varying the parameters of the model, specifically exploring the two-dimensional parameter space at a fixed quartic coupling. The authors also employ weak and strong coupling expansions for consistency checks.

Result: The results reveal a massless symmetric phase, a massive symmetric phase (SMG), and an intermediate anti-ferromagnetic phase. A multicritical point is found where all fermion bilinear condensates disappear and fermion susceptibilities show non-trivial power-law divergence with the lattice size.

Conclusion: The conclusion is that the phase structure of the lattice Higgs model exhibits rich behavior, including a multicritical point, which may be associated with a phase of topological defect condensation, providing a deeper understanding of the underlying physics.

Abstract: We conduct numerical simulations to map out the phase diagram and critical behavior of a lattice Higgs model composed of two massless staggered fermion fields forming a doublet under a global $SU(2)$ and coupled to a scalar field in the adjoint representation of the group. The scalar action consists of a potential comprising quadratic and quartic terms and a scalar kinetic term. At fixed quartic coupling we explore a two-dimensional parameter space finding a massless symmetric phase at weak coupling and a massive symmetric phase (SMG phase) at strong coupling. An intermediate anti-ferromagnetic phase separates these two regimes. These results are consistent with leading order weak and strong coupling expansions. We find that the critical lines bounding the intermediate phase merge at a unique point where all fermion bilinear condensates vanish but fermion susceptibilities diverge as non-trivial powers of the lattice size. We conjecture that this merged point corresponds to a multicritical point and may describe a phase consisting of a condensate of certain topological defects.
