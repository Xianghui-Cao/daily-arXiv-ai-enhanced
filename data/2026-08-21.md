### [1] [The QCD crossover temperature and curvature coefficient from unbiased exponential resummation at physical quark masses in (2+1)-flavor lattice QCD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19330)
*Sabarnya Mitra*

Main category: hep-lat

TL;DR: This paper applies the unbiased exponential resummation method to determine the QCD pseudocritical temperature at zero baryon chemical potential. It uses baryon number susceptibility and its thermal-derivative observables, finding consistent results with previous lattice-QCD studies and demonstrating the consistency of the method with expected scaling behavior.

Motivation: The motivation is to apply the unbiased exponential resummation method for the first time to determine the QCD pseudocritical temperature (T_pc) at vanishing baryon chemical potential (μ_B), and to confirm its consistency with expected scaling behavior and other methods such as lattice-QCD results based on Taylor expansions.

Method: The method involves reconstructing the finite-density baryon number susceptibility (χ_2^B) and defining two thermal-derivative observables whose peak positions provide T_pc. The authors also analyze the fourth-order baryon number susceptibility (χ_4^B) and use scaling relations between χ_4^B and the thermal derivatives of χ_2^B to estimate the leading second order curvature coefficient (κ_2^B).

Result: The results show that the pseudocritical temperatures obtained from the different observables are consistent with each other and with previous lattice-QCD results. The scaling relations between χ_4^B and the thermal derivatives of χ_2^B yield mutually consistent estimates of κ_2^B. An independent analysis of the μ_B dependence of the pseudocritical temperatures provides a less constrained but statistically consistent determination of the curvature.

Conclusion: The unbiased exponential resummation method is found to be consistent with the expected scaling behavior and established as a complementary approach for probing the QCD crossover at small finite baryon density. The consistency of the determined pseudocritical temperatures with those from other methods supports the reliability of the new approach.

Abstract: We present the first application of the unbiased exponential resummation method to the determination of the QCD pseudocritical temperature $T_{\rm pc}$ at vanishing baryon chemical potential $μ_B$. By reconstructing the finite-density baryon number susceptibility $χ_2^B$, we define two thermal-derivative observables whose peak positions yield $T_{\rm pc}^T=160.2(4)(3)$ MeV and $T_{\rm pc}^C=158.1(5)(3)$ MeV. We also show that the pseudocritical temperature, $T_{\rm pc}^χ$ obtained from the peak of the fourth-order baryon number susceptibility $χ_4^B$ is consistent with these determinations and with previous lattice-QCD results based on Taylor expansions \cite{Bollweg:2022Pade}. Further, we demonstrate that scaling relations \cite{Bollweg:2022fqq} between $χ_4^B$ and the thermal derivatives of $χ_2^B$ yield mutually consistent estimates of the leading second order curvature coefficient $κ_2^B$ at the corresponding pseudocritical temperatures. A direct analysis of the $μ_B$ dependence of the pseudocritical temperatures provides an independent but substantially less constrained determination of the curvature, which remains statistically consistent with the scaling-based estimates. These results demonstrate the consistency of unbiased exponential resummation with the expected scaling behavior and establish it as a complementary approach for probing the QCD crossover at small finite baryon density.
