### [1] [Long-distance hybrid spin-dependent and hybrid-quarkonium mixing potentials from lattice gauge theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00266)
*Vilija de Jonge,Paul Pütz,Antonio Vairo,Marc Wagner*

Main category: hep-lat

TL;DR: This paper presents new lattice gauge theory results for specific potentials, extending the range of quark-antiquark separations previously studied, using high-precision simulations.


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: The motivation is to extend the existing lattice computation of hybrid spin-dependent and hybrid-quarkonium mixing potentials to a larger range of quark-antiquark separations, which has not been explored before, in order to provide more comprehensive data for these interactions.

Method: The method involves large-volume, high-statistics simulations at several lattice spacings and gradient flow times, enabling the study of quark-antiquark separations up to approximately 1.25 fm, which is significantly larger than the 0.5 fm limit of previous studies.

Result: The result is the first set of lattice data for the specified potentials at quark-antiquark separations beyond 0.5 fm, with data now available up to around 1.25 fm, providing new insights into the behavior of these potentials over a wider range of distances.

Conclusion: The conclusion is that this work successfully extends the knowledge of the $\mathcal{O}(1/m_Q)$ hybrid spin-dependent and hybrid-quarkonium mixing potentials, offering valuable data for future theoretical and experimental studies in the field of lattice QCD.

Abstract: We present $SU(3)$ lattice gauge theory results for the $\mathcal{O}(1/m_Q)$ hybrid spin-dependent and hybrid-quarkonium mixing potentials. The only existing lattice computation of these potentials is limited to quark-antiquark separations smaller than approximately $0.5\,\text{fm}$. In this work, we extend the lattice results up to around $1.25 \, \text{fm}$ using large-volume, high-statistics simulations at several lattice spacings and gradient flow times, providing the first lattice data in this previously unexplored regime.
