### [1] [Hamiltonian framework for Chiral Gauge Theories on a Disk Boundary](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11035)
*Srimoyee Sen*

Main category: hep-lat

TL;DR: A new Hamiltonian framework for chiral gauge theories (CGT) is proposed, which uses 2n dimensional chiral fermions on a 2n+1 dimensional disk's boundary. This approach overcomes the limitations of the original Euclidean formulation by providing a way to extend the gauge fields into the bulk that is compatible with both a Hamiltonian framework and a Euclidean path integral, thus preserving non-perturbative content and potentially enabling quantum simulation of CGTs.

Motivation: The motivation behind this paper is to develop a Hamiltonian framework for chiral gauge theories that can be used for future quantum simulations. The existing Euclidean formulation has a bottleneck due to the method of extending the gauge fields into the bulk, which is not compatible with constructing a Hamiltonian. The author aims to resolve this issue and to preserve the non-perturbative properties of the theory, such as topological charge behavior and the absence of the strong CP problem in the Standard Model context.

Method: The method introduced in this paper involves an alternative proposal for extending Abelian gauge fields into the bulk. This new extension is designed to be consistent with both a Hamiltonian framework and the Euclidean path integral. The author provides exact expressions for interior fields as a function of the boundary fields, which are suitable for use in a Hamiltonian formulation. The method is also analyzed within the Euclidean framework to ensure that it retains the non-perturbative features of the original construction.

Result: The result is a viable method for extending the gauge fields into the bulk, which allows for the construction of a Hamiltonian. The new approach maintains the non-perturbative aspects, such as the correct behavior of the topological charge, the associated chiral fermion zero modes, and the resolution of the strong CP problem. It also paves the way for the potential quantum simulation of chiral gauge theories on a disk boundary.

Conclusion: The conclusion drawn from this work is that the newly proposed method for extending the gauge fields into the bulk resolves the previous issues with the Hamiltonian formulation while maintaining the non-perturbative characteristics of the chiral gauge theories. This development opens up possibilities for further theoretical exploration and practical application, including the simulation of these theories in a quantum setting.

Abstract: I propose a Hamiltonian framework for chiral gauge theories (CGT) based on a Euclidean formulation which uses 2n dimensional chiral fermions on the boundary of a 2n+1 dimensional disk. In the original Euclidean formulation, boundary gauge fields were extended into the bulk using $2n + 1$ dimensional gauge field equations of motion (EOM) which creates a bottleneck for constructing a Hamiltonian. I present an alternate proposal for extending the gauge fields into the bulk that is compatible with both a Hamiltonian framework and a Euclidean path integral. Applying this to Abelian gauge fields produces exact expressions of interior fields as a functional of the boundary fields, which can be directly used in a Hamiltonian formulation. Euclidean analysis of the new gauge field extension shows that it can preserve the non-perturbative content of the original construction, including the behavior of topological charge, associated chiral fermion zero modes and an absence of the strong CP problem when applied to the Standard Model. This construction opens up a route to a Hamiltonian treatment and future quantum simulation of CGTs on a disk boundary.
