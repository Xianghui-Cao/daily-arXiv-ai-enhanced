### [1] [The QCD crossover temperature and curvature coefficient from unbiased exponential resummation at physical quark masses in (2+1)-flavor lattice QCD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19330)
*Sabarnya Mitra*

Main category: hep-lat

TL;DR: The paper introduces the use of an unbiased exponential resummation method to determine the QCD pseudocritical temperature at zero baryon chemical potential, finding consistent results with previous lattice-QCD studies and validating the method as a viable approach for investigating the QCD crossover at small finite baryon density.

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: The motivation is to apply the unbiased exponential resummation method for the first time to determine the QCD pseudocritical temperature and to verify its consistency with expected scaling behaviors and previous lattice-QCD results, particularly in the context of small finite baryon density.

Method: The authors reconstruct the finite-density baryon number susceptibility and define two thermal-derivative observables. The peak positions of these observables are used to determine the pseudocritical temperatures. Additionally, they analyze the fourth-order baryon number susceptibility and use scaling relations to estimate the leading second order curvature coefficient, while also assessing the mu_B dependence of the pseudocritical temperatures.

Result: The study yields pseudocritical temperatures T_pc^T = 160.2(4)(3) MeV and T_pc^C = 158.1(5)(3) MeV, which are consistent with each other and with previous lattice-QCD results. The analysis of the fourth-order baryon number susceptibility and scaling relations provide consistent estimates of the curvature coefficient, and the direct analysis of the mu_B dependence gives a less constrained but statistically consistent determination of the curvature.

Conclusion: The unbiased exponential resummation method provides consistent determinations of the QCD pseudocritical temperature and the curvature coefficient, supporting its use as a complementary method for studying the QCD crossover at small finite baryon density.

Abstract: We present the first application of the unbiased exponential resummation method to the determination of the QCD pseudocritical temperature $T_{\rm pc}$ at vanishing baryon chemical potential $μ_B$. By reconstructing the finite-density baryon number susceptibility $χ_2^B$, we define two thermal-derivative observables whose peak positions yield $T_{\rm pc}^T=160.2(4)(3)$ MeV and $T_{\rm pc}^C=158.1(5)(3)$ MeV. We also show that the pseudocritical temperature, $T_{\rm pc}^χ$ obtained from the peak of the fourth-order baryon number susceptibility $χ_4^B$ is consistent with these determinations and with previous lattice-QCD results based on Taylor expansions \cite{Bollweg:2022Pade}. Further, we demonstrate that scaling relations \cite{Bollweg:2022fqq} between $χ_4^B$ and the thermal derivatives of $χ_2^B$ yield mutually consistent estimates of the leading second order curvature coefficient $κ_2^B$ at the corresponding pseudocritical temperatures. A direct analysis of the $μ_B$ dependence of the pseudocritical temperatures provides an independent but substantially less constrained determination of the curvature, which remains statistically consistent with the scaling-based estimates. These results demonstrate the consistency of unbiased exponential resummation with the expected scaling behavior and establish it as a complementary approach for probing the QCD crossover at small finite baryon density.
