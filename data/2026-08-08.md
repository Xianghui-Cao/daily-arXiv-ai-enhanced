### [1] [QCD Chiral Crossover Line from Lee-Yang Edge Singularities](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05752)
*Heng-Tong Ding,Swagato Mukherjee,Peter Petreczky,Kai-Fan Ye*

Main category: hep-lat

TL;DR: The paper introduces a method to reconstruct the QCD chiral crossover line using Lee-Yang edge singularities, which is validated with data from the Wuppertal-Budapest collaboration. This approach, when combined with universal chiral scaling, provides a consistent constraint on the QCD crossover line and can be refined with future data.

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: To develop a universality-based approach for reconstructing the QCD chiral crossover line as a function of baryon chemical potential, utilizing lattice-extracted Lee-Yang zeros as a proxy for edge singularities in the complex plane.

Method: A framework that maps the lattice-extracted complex Lee-Yang-zero estimates onto the universal chiral Lee-Yang edge, allowing the determination of the $\mu_B$ dependence of the chiral critical line and the pseudo-critical crossover line at physical quark masses. The method was applied to recent high-statistics lattice QCD simulation results from the Wuppertal-Budapest collaboration.

Result: The reconstructed curvature of the crossover line, without using the small-$\mu_B$ expansion as an input, agrees with existing continuum lattice-QCD results at small $\mu_B$. The chiral-limit transition temperature derived from the fit is also consistent with other chiral-scaling analyses.

Conclusion: The combination of lattice information on Lee-Yang singularities with universal chiral scaling offers a quantitatively consistent constraint on the QCD crossover line, and the proposed framework can be systematically improved with additional Lee-Yang-zero determinations.

Abstract: We propose a universality-based reconstruction of the QCD chiral crossover line from Lee-Yang edge singularities in the complex baryon chemical potential plane. The framework maps lattice-extracted complex Lee-Yang-zero estimates, treated as proxies for edge singularities, to the universal chiral Lee-Yang edge and thereby determines the $μ_B$ dependence of both the chiral critical line in the light-quark chiral limit and the pseudo-critical crossover line at physical quark masses. As an illustration, we apply the framework to Lee-Yang-zero estimates recently obtained by the Wuppertal-Budapest collaboration from high-statistics lattice QCD simulations. Without imposing the previously determined small-$μ_B$ expansion of the crossover line as input, the reconstructed curvature is consistent with existing continuum lattice-QCD results at small $μ_B$. The fitted chiral-limit transition temperature is also compatible with existing chiral-scaling analyses. These results demonstrate that lattice information on Lee-Yang singularities, combined with universal chiral scaling, provides a quantitatively consistent constraint on the QCD crossover line within the present temperature window and establishes a framework that can be systematically improved with future Lee-Yang-zero determinations.
